## 1. What was done

Starting point: the graph answered one of a trade's four questions (direction).
It now answers the other three — **how big, how much is already priced in, and
how much to trust it** — and turns detected manipulation/emotion into
positions instead of only abstaining.

### 1a. Engine math (`brain/graph.py`)

- **Regime-gated edges** (`Edge.regime_gate`, 15 curated): relationships that
  are only true in one regime flip/mute/damp outside their band. Fed↔risk
  flips in growth scares; yields↔banks flips in panics (SVB); melt-ups shrug
  at war/antitrust/fraud headlines; the halving narrative mutes in risk-off.
- **Crisis correlation convergence**: in deep risk-off, membership/correlation
  weights bend toward 1 — diversification dies exactly when it's needed.
- **Market anticipation of τ-lags**: delayed edges into PRICED destinations
  land half now, half on schedule; real-economy destinations (CPI, growth)
  stay fully deferred.
- **Evidence multipliers**: calibration verdicts adjust edge confidence in
  memory at adjacency-build time — never persisted, can't compound.
- `_merge_seed` now refreshes curated node metadata, so label/alias/sign
  convention fixes actually reach deployed graph files.

### 1b. Scale, surprise, proof

- `brain/scale.py` — per-symbol daily vol (realized from snapshots when ≥15
  obs, honest per-market priors otherwise) turns impacts into **expected
  moves** (`impact × vol × √h × gain`). Adviser sizes risk, not conviction.
- `brain/priced_in.py` — signals the tape already ran on get discounted (up
  to 80% at a 3σ run), **volume-weighted**: heavy tape = information consumed
  = bigger discount; thin tape = drift.
- `brain/calibration.py` — every curated influences-edge AND every
  theme→member path scored against realized ~5d forward returns
  (n / hit-rate / t-stat / verdict). Supported ×1.15, contradicted ×0.5,
  unproven left honest. Global `gain` corrects expected-move overshoot.
  Runs INSIDE the cycle whenever fresh outcomes land; re-weights the live
  graph immediately. CLI: `python3 -m ai_investing.brain.calibration`.

### 1c. The bullshit/emotion layer (detection → offense)

- `brain/emotion_field.py` — per-node fear/greed charges, 48h half-life.
  Noise charges greed at half weight (hype noise IS signal); noise can never
  charge fear (spam can't fake capitulation). Assets inherit theme emotion.
- `brain/campaign.py` — manipulation-pressure index per node (mention burst +
  low-trust chorus + coordinated timing + noise mass) with **pump lifecycle**
  staging (building / hype_burst / dump) and volume confirmation. Fading only
  allowed in `dump`. Adviser haircuts fresh longs by pressure.
- `brain/source_learning.py` — events ≥5d old are replayed through the graph
  and scored vs realized moves (`event_outcomes` table). Per-source learned
  trust blends 50/50 with the static prior at n≥10. **Doom discount**: a feed
  whose fear stories never move markets gets its fear impulses damped.
- `brain/emotion_calibration.py` — "buy when others are fearful" is measured,
  not assumed: post-panic / post-euphoria forward-return stats become
  contrarian coefficients (honest ±0.30 priors, labeled, until n≥20).
- `brain/contrarian.py` — the offense:
  * **BUY** panic × clean integrity × outside money-circles × value case ×
    stabilization gate (still-falling names go to WATCHING, zero boost).
  * **FADE** euphoria × (froth | circularity | campaign pressure),
    lifecycle-gated — never fade a pump that's working.
  * **BENEFICIARIES**: an integrity flag on X tilts X's `competes_with`
    neighbors positive (Luckin's fraud = Starbucks China's share).
- `events.py` hardened — corroboration counts only TRUSTED sources; a chorus
  of low-trust feeds echoing one story now *penalizes* credibility
  (coordination is not confirmation). Learned trust threads through both.

### 1e. Chain-integrity audit (all loops verified closed)

1. Edge/path calibrator chained into the cycle (was CLI-only — it would have
   collected evidence forever and never acted).
2. Regime updates BEFORE propagation — gates see today's dials, same cycle.
3. Bullshit-layer failures surface as `state["layer_error"]` — degrade
   gracefully, never silently.
4. Learned source trust flows into corroboration and the campaign chorus.
5. Strategist evidence pack includes contrarian lists, live campaigns,
   per-node emotion, and reflex-calibration status.
6. Membership map precomputed (perf).

Tests: `tests/test_brain_upgrades.py` (21) + `tests/test_bullshit_layer.py`
(15) including three chain-integrity tests. Full suite green (`make test`).

### 1f. Honest limits (deliberate, labeled — not oversights)

- Anticipation 0.5, convergence κ 0.6, gate thresholds: priors, on record,
  awaiting calibration.
- Emotion coefficients are global (not per-asset-class/regime) until
  `event_outcomes` can support finer slices without fitting noise.
- Stress scenarios and outcome scoring propagate regime-free by design.
- `simulate()` doesn't run the bullshit layer (what-if must not mutate state).
- **Every evidence loop is data-starved until history accumulates** (§3).

## 3. What's next (priority order)

1. **Turn it on and leave it on.** Every loop feeds on daily cycles
   (node_history, price_history, event_outcomes). Cron the engine if it
   isn't already. Nothing else matters more.
2. **Backfill via replay** once §2b lands: `research/news_replay.py` /
   `replay_fetch_data.py`, then `python3 -m ai_investing.brain.calibration`
   for the first real verdict report on the 621 edges and 15 gates.
3. **Human review of the first calibration report**: contradicted edges
   (wiring wrong vs unrepresentative sample?), the 15 gates (falsifiable
   claims — did the flips show up?), and the `gain` number.
4. **Close the action gap.** The offense stops at the adviser (decision
   support). Recommended: let `MacroLinkageSignal` (or a small new signal)
   consume contrarian scores + campaign pressure so they pass through the
   walk-forward gate, risk caps, and safety stack — the walk-forward test
   gets to veto the offense. Also confirm whether the broker path can short;
   if not, "fade" honestly means avoid/exit/never-chase.
5. **Paper-grade the offense first**: let contrarian buy/fade lists run in
   the advice log 30–60 days; split their hit-rate from ordinary field calls
   (all in `advice_log`/`advice_outcomes`). Gate #4 on ~55%+ directional.
6. **Dashboard + alerts**: `brain.json` now carries `emotion_field`,
   `campaigns`, `contrarian`, `calibration`, `layer_error` — render them;
   campaign detections and contrarian triggers should ping Telegram.
   `layer_error` must be impossible to miss.
7. **Then new capability**: positioning data (CFTC COT, short interest,
   options skew — crowded fear vs fresh fear), per-regime emotion
   coefficients, narrative objects (story age/consensus).
